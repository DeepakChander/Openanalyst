## Sharp Edges (Gotchas)

*Real production issues that cause outages and bugs.*

### [MEDIUM] AI designs all look similar

**Situation:** Generated designs lack brand uniqueness

**Why it happens:**
AI trained on similar data, produces similar outputs

**Solution:**
```
Use AI for speed, add unique brand elements
```

---

### [HIGH] AI image licensing can be unclear

**Situation:** Using AI images commercially

**Why it happens:**
Training data may include copyrighted images

**Solution:**
```
Use Adobe Firefly or similar with clear licensing
```

---

### [MEDIUM] AI designs may not be accessible

**Situation:** Generated UI lacks accessibility

**Why it happens:**
AI doesn't prioritize a11y

**Solution:**
```
Always check contrast, screen reader compatibility
```

---

### [MEDIUM] AI doesn't know your brand

**Situation:** Generated designs don't match brand

**Why it happens:**
No brand context unless provided

**Solution:**
```
Provide brand guidelines, color palettes
```
