## Sharp Edges (Gotchas)

*Real production issues that cause outages and bugs.*

### [HIGH] Recording meetings raises privacy concerns

**Situation:** Recording without consent

**Why it happens:**
Legal requirements vary, trust issues with clients

**Solution:**
```
Always get consent, announce at meeting start
```

---

### [MEDIUM] AI scheduling can create conflicts

**Situation:** AI moves meetings unexpectedly

**Why it happens:**
AI optimizes without human context

**Solution:**
```
Lock important meetings, review changes
```

---

### [MEDIUM] AI email drafts may not match your voice

**Situation:** AI draft sounds generic or wrong

**Why it happens:**
AI doesn't know your relationships

**Solution:**
```
Always review before sending
```

---

### [MEDIUM] Transcription mistakes in important notes

**Situation:** Key terms or names transcribed wrong

**Why it happens:**
Accents, jargon, technical terms

**Solution:**
```
Review action items, add custom vocabulary
```

---

### [MEDIUM] AI fills every minute, no buffer

**Situation:** Back-to-back meetings, no breaks

**Why it happens:**
AI optimizes for time use, not energy

**Solution:**
```
Configure buffer time, protect breaks
```
