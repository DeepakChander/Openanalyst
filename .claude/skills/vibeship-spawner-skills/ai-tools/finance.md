## Sharp Edges (Gotchas)

*Real production issues that cause outages and bugs.*

### [HIGH] AI miscategorizes transactions

**Situation:** AI learns from limited data

**Why it happens:**
Financial data is nuanced, categories overlap

**Solution:**
```
Review AI suggestions, train with corrections, audit monthly
```

**Symptoms:**
- Expenses in wrong categories
- Tax deductions missed
- Reports inaccurate

---

### [MEDIUM] Bank connections break or lag

**Situation:** Bank APIs change, connections drop

**Why it happens:**
Banks update security, third-party aggregators fail

**Solution:**
```
Monitor connections, have backup import process
```

**Symptoms:**
- Missing transactions
- Duplicate entries after reconnect
- Reconciliation gaps

---

### [HIGH] Data doesn't sync between tools

**Situation:** Multi-tool finance stack

**Why it happens:**
Different data models, timing issues

**Solution:**
```
Single source of truth, audit sync regularly
```

**Symptoms:**
- Numbers don't match across systems
- Duplicate data
- Missing records

---

### [CRITICAL] AI forecasts treated as certainty

**Situation:** AI generates confident predictions

**Why it happens:**
Models extrapolate from limited data

**Solution:**
```
Always use scenarios, understand assumptions
```

**Symptoms:**
- Missed runway
- Over-hiring based on projections
- Board confusion when actuals differ

---

### [CRITICAL] AI doesn't catch all tax rules

**Situation:** Tax laws are complex and local

**Why it happens:**
AI can't know every jurisdiction, every exception

**Solution:**
```
Work with tax professional, don't rely solely on AI
```

**Symptoms:**
- Nexus violations
- Incorrect tax rates
- Audit exposure

---

### [MEDIUM] Employees game AI expense rules

**Situation:** AI enforces policies automatically

**Why it happens:**
People learn to phrase things to pass AI checks

**Solution:**
```
Random audits, manager reviews, clear policies
```

**Symptoms:**
- Unusual spending patterns
- Creative expense descriptions
- Policy violations that slip through

### [MEDIUM] Receipt scanning makes mistakes

**Situation:** AI reads receipts automatically

**Why it happens:**
Poor image quality, unusual formats

**Solution:**
```
Verify large expenses, spot-check regularly
```

**Symptoms:**
- Wrong amounts
- Incorrect vendors
- Missing tax details

### [HIGH] Currency conversion causes issues

**Situation:** International business

**Why it happens:**
Exchange rates fluctuate, timing matters

**Solution:**
```
Consistent rate sources, understand gain/loss
```

**Symptoms:**
- Unexpected forex gains/losses
- Reconciliation nightmares
- Reporting inconsistencies

### [HIGH] AI changes without documentation

**Situation:** AI auto-corrects or adjusts

**Why it happens:**
Automation can obscure what happened

**Solution:**
```
Ensure full audit logs, review AI actions
```

**Symptoms:**

- Historical data looks different

### [CRITICAL] AI calculates runway wrong

**Situation:** Cash runway is critical metric

AI may not account for timing, commitments

Manual runway check, include all obligations
