|-------|-------------|

- Response time targets

## Anti-Patterns

### Marketing to Developers
Treating developers like traditional marketing targets
**Why it's bad:** Developers detect and reject marketing.
Trust is destroyed instantly.
Word spreads in developer circles.


### Ignoring Contributor Friction
Making it hard to contribute
**Why it's bad:** Potential contributors give up.
Community stays small.
Maintainer burnout.


### Vanity DevRel Metrics
Measuring followers instead of adoption
**Why it's bad:** Followers don't equal developers building.
Can't tie to business value.
Wrong incentives.
