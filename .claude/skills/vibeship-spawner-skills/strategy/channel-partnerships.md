```
## Partner Program Design

### 1. Partner Types

| Type | Value Add | Your Investment | Best For |
|------|-----------|-----------------|----------|
| Resellers | Distribution, local presence | Lower touch | Volume, SMB |

### 2. Program Tiers

```
Tier 1: Premier Partners
- Revenue: Top 10%
- Commitment: Dedicated resources
- Benefits: Best margins, co-selling, MDF
- Requirements: Certification, minimums

Tier 2: Advanced Partners
- Revenue: Next 20%
- Commitment: Regular engagement
- Benefits: Good margins, leads, support
- Requirements: Training, quarterly review

Tier 3: Registered Partners
- Revenue: Remaining 70%
- Commitment: Self-serve
- Benefits: Base margins, portal access
- Requirements: Agreement, basic training
```

### 3. Tier Benefits Matrix

| Benefit | Registered | Advanced | Premier |
|---------|------------|----------|---------|
| Discount/Margin | 15% | 25% | 35% |
| Deal registration | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Protected margin | ✗ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Co-marketing funds | ✗ | $2K/Q | $10K/Q |
| Dedicated PM | ✗ | Shared | Dedicated |
| Lead sharing | ✗ | ✓ | Priority |
| Executive access | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ |

### 4. Partner Economics

```
Partner Revenue Share Model:

Option A: Resale Discount
- Partner buys at 30% discount
- Partner sets end-customer price
- Simple, clear

Option B: Revenue Share
- Partner earns 20% of deal
- You bill customer
- Maintains pricing control

Option C: Referral Fee
- Partner earns 10-15% of first year
- You own customer relationship
- Lower commitment for partners
```

| Advanced | Required | 2 people | $50K/year | Quarterly |
```

```
- Prevents channel conflict

```

- 48-hour approval SLA
- Protection period: 90 days
- Renewal: Every 90 days

- First to register wins
- Customer preference considered
- Escalation to channel chief

## Anti-Patterns

### Partner Warehouse
Signing partners but not activating them
**Why it's bad:** Dead partners hurt reputation.
Dilutes focus from productive ones.
Waste of recruitment effort.


### Channel Conflict Ignorance
Not managing direct vs channel overlap
**Why it's bad:** Partners lose trust.
Price undercutting.
Damaged relationships.


### Margin-Only Motivation
Relying on margins to motivate partners
**Why it's bad:** Easy to sell beats high margin.
Partners have many products to sell.
Margin doesn't solve capability gaps.


### Set and Forget
Launching program without ongoing investment
**Why it's bad:** Partners disengage quickly.
Competitors steal attention.
Program becomes zombie.


### One-Size-Fits-All
Same program for all partner types
**Why it's bad:** Agencies have different needs than VARs.
Misaligned incentives.
Poor partner experience.

```
## Activating Dead Partners

### Diagnosis

**Partner Health Audit**
```
Active: Revenue in last 90 days
Engaged: Activity in last 90 days (training, portal, etc.)
Dormant: No activity in 90+ days
Dead: No activity in 180+ days

Healthy program: >50% active
Problem program: <30% active
```

**Tier 1: High Potential Dormant**
1. Executive outreach
2. Understand barriers
3. Dedicated success manager
4. Co-investment offer

**Tier 2: Medium Potential**

2. Offer incentive/SPIF
3. Invite to partner event
4. Provide qualified leads

**Tier 3: Low Potential**
1. Final notice
2. Downgrade tier
3. Consider termination
4. Free up resources

**Activation Requirements**
```

Partner not "signed" until:
- Training complete (30 days)

### [HIGH] Direct and channel competing for same deals

**Situation:** Internal sales and partners fighting over accounts

**Why it happens:**
No clear rules of engagement.
Deal registration not enforced.

**Solution:**
```
## Channel Conflict Resolution

### Root Cause Analysis

| Conflict Type | Cause | Solution |
|---------------|-------|----------|
| Price undercutting | No price parity | Enforce price rules |
| Account overlap | No territory rules | Clear account assignment |
| Deal stealing | No deal reg | Enforce registration |
| Services conflict | Unclear scope | Define partner vs direct |

### Rules of Engagement

**Account Assignment**
```
Named accounts: Direct owns
Territory accounts: First to engage owns
Partner-sourced: Partner owns
Inbound leads: Route by criteria
```

**Deal Registration**
```
Valid registration:
- Submitted before customer contact
- Customer confirms partner relationship
- Protection period: 90 days
- Renewable once

Conflict resolution:
- First valid registration wins
- Customer preference breaks ties
- Channel chief final arbiter
```

**Price Parity**
```
Partner price ≥ Direct price - Partner margin

Example:
Direct price: $10,000
Partner margin: 20%
Partner minimum price: $10,000 × 0.8 = $8,000

No undercutting below partner floor
```

### Conflict Escalation

| Level | Resolution Time | Arbiter |
|-------|-----------------|---------|
| 1 | 24 hours | Channel manager |
| 2 | 48 hours | Channel director |
| 3 | 72 hours | CRO/Channel chief |

### Compensation Alignment

```
Direct rep on partner deal:
- Full quota credit
- Reduced commission (50%)
- Encourage partner support

Partner on direct deal:
- Referral fee only
- No margin
- Clear expectations
```

```

**Symptoms:**
- Sales and partners both claiming deals
